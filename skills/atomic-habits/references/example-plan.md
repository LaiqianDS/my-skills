# Example: a filled plan

One worked plan, for the case "I have tried the gym four times this year and it never lasts past week two".
Copy the shape, never the content.
What makes it a plan and not a summary is that every line carries a time, a place, or the user's own words.

---

# Habit plan: strength training

## Identity
I am someone who trains.
Each session is a vote for it, and the two-minute version votes the same as the full hour.

## The loop
- **Cue**: 18:30, at the gym two streets from the office, straight after shutting the laptop
- **Craving**: to stop feeling like the week ran me
- **Response**: the printed programme, three lifts
- **Reward**: ten minutes in the sauna before going home

## The floor
Get changed and do one set of squats.
This is the version for the worst day, and it counts as a full repetition.

## Techniques
Broken stage: reward, because "week two is when I stop, I never see anything in the mirror so it feels pointless".
- **Attach an immediate reward**: the sauna is only allowed after training, never on its own. The payoff moves from three months away to ten minutes away.
- **Keep the reward from fighting the goal**: the sauna costs nothing extra and needs no recovery, so it does not undo the session it is paying for.

## The ladder
| Rung | Version | Unlocks when |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Changed, one set of squats | now |
| 1 | The three lifts, no time target | two weeks with no two sessions missed in a row |
| 2 | The full programme, three evenings a week | four weeks at rung 1, with the third evening added and the first two still standing |

## Maintenance
- **Never miss twice**: two scheduled evenings in a row. A missed Tuesday is not a break; missing Tuesday and Thursday is.
- **Metric**: weight moved on the main lift each week, not weight on the scale, which climbs just as well on a bad diet.
- **Review**: 2026-09-07, against this document.

## Assumptions
- Three evenings a week is assumed from "I want to go regularly". Not confirmed.
- The sauna is assumed to be part of the membership.

## Log
- 2026-08-24: plan written

---

## What the same file looks like after one review

The document is rewritten in place, not replaced.
Three things move and nothing else does:

- **The ladder**: rung 1 becomes the current version, because the two weeks held. A rung is added on top so there is still something above.
- **Assumptions**: the line about three evenings goes, because the review answered it. What is still unconfirmed stays.
- **Log**: one line is appended, `2026-09-07: rung 0 held four weeks running, moved to rung 1`.

The identity line does not move unless the user changes it.
That is the point of it.
